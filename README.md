# UX-research-questions
Research questions are not interview questions. You can't just ask users what you want to find out – put your detective hat on and conduct a proper investigation

## What this skill is and is not
Helps UX researchers define, sharpen, and match research questions to the right method. Use this skill whenever the you want to frame a research study, turn a business problem into a research question, scope a research initiative, or choose a research method.


## Installing the [UX Research Questions] skill
1. Download the skill:
- Clone repo: `git clone https://github.com/abraslasu/UX-research-questions`
- Or download ZIP from Releases
2. Install in Claude:
- Open Claude.ai > Settings > skills
- Click "Upload skill"
